HalalScan Support

Welcome to HalalScan Support. HalalScan helps users scan product barcodes and review ingredient information for an ingredient-based halal screening.

How to Scan a Product

  1. Open HalalScan.
  2. Tap Scan Product.
  3. Allow camera access if requested.
  4. Point the camera at the product barcode.
  5. Wait for the product information and screening result.

Manual Barcode Search

You can also enter a product barcode manually in the barcode field and tap Search Barcode.

Understanding Results

HALAL
No obvious haram ingredients were detected based on the available ingredient information.

MASHBOOH (UNCERTAIN)
One or more ingredients may have an unclear source. Check the ingredient source or official halal certification.

NOT HALAL
A prohibited ingredient or product was detected based on the available information.

UNABLE TO CHECK
HalalScan could not retrieve sufficient product information. Try again later or check the product ingredients yourself.

Scan History

Your recent scan results can be viewed in Scan History. You can remove your saved scan history using the Clear History option.

Important Information

HalalScan provides an ingredient-based screening result. It does not replace official halal certification. Always review the ingredients and official halal certification when appropriate.
