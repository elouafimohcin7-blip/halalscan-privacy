HalalScan Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

HalalScan ("HalalScan", "we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the HalalScan mobile application.

1. Information We Access

Camera

HalalScan requests access to your device camera so you can scan product barcodes. HalalScan does not use the camera to identify you, record video, or collect photographs for advertising purposes.

Barcode Information

When you scan or manually enter a barcode, HalalScan may send the barcode number to third-party product databases to retrieve product information such as product name, brand, ingredients, and product category.

2. Scan History

HalalScan may save your scanned product results on your device so you can view your previous scans. Your scan history is stored locally on your device. You can delete your scan history using the Clear History option within the app.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

HalalScan does not intentionally collect:

  • Your name
  • Your home address
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts
  • Your photographs
  • Your precise location
  • Your financial information
  • Your passwords

HalalScan does not sell personal information to advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

HalalScan may use third-party product databases, including Open Food Facts, to obtain product and ingredient information. When a barcode lookup is performed, the barcode may be transmitted to these services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. How HalalScan Uses Information

Information accessed by HalalScan is used to:

  • Scan product barcodes
  • Retrieve product information
  • Analyze ingredient information
  • Provide an ingredient-based halal screening result
  • Display and maintain your local scan history

6. Halal Screening

HalalScan provides an ingredient-based screening result and is not a halal certification authority.

Results may be classified as:

  • HALAL
  • MASHBOOH (UNCERTAIN)
  • NOT HALAL
  • UNABLE TO CHECK

Users should verify ingredients and official halal certification when appropriate.

7. Children's Privacy

HalalScan is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic service can guarantee complete security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HalalScan's features or services change. The updated version will include a new effective date.
